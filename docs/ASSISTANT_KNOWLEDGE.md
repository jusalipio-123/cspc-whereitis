# Ask WhereITis: knowledge base

Facts about CSPC WhereITis, the Lost & Found system of Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges (CSPC). The assistant answers from this file and from live lookups only. Each "##" section is one chunk; keep chunks short and factual.

## Core facts
WhereITis helps CSPC students and staff reunite lost items with their owners. It is a coordination tool and does not guarantee that an item is recovered. It handles no payments, fines or fees. Main pages: Home (/), Reports board (/reports), Report an item (/reports/new), Nexus (/nexus), Help (/help), My Reports (/my-reports), Messages (/messages), Notifications (/notifications), Privacy (/privacy), Terms (/terms). Report reference numbers look like LF-2026-0007.

## Report a lost item
Needs an account. Click Report an item (/reports/new?kind=lost) and choose "I lost something". Give a short title such as "Black leather wallet", pick a category and the location where it was last seen, enter the date lost (not in the future), and describe it in at least 20 characters: colour, brand, marks. A photo can be added (one photo, up to 5 MB). After submitting you get a reference number like LF-2026-0007. The report is public right away and the matching scanner compares it against found reports.

## Report a found item
Anyone can report a found item, with or without an account (/reports/new?kind=found). Fill in the same fields, and in the Holding location box say where the item is being kept, for example "CCS Dean's office, 2nd floor". Note the reference number. A registered user's report is public immediately. A guest report (no account) is private until staff review and approve it, and guests can send only a few reports per day. Guest contact details are optional, visible to staff only, and never shown publicly.

## Guest or account
Guests can report a found item only. An account is needed to report a lost item, file a claim, send messages, get notifications, track reports, and edit or withdraw reports.

## Search for an item
Use the search box on the home page or open Reports (/reports). Type a keyword such as a colour, brand or part of a name. Filters: lost or found, category, location, status, date range. Press Enter or Apply filters. The Nexus match scanner (/nexus) can also search by a free description.

## File a claim
Open the found report you think is yours and click "This is mine - file a claim". You must be signed in. Write at least 30 characters of proof (where and when you lost it, how you would recognise it) and at least 30 characters of features only the true owner would know. Submit to see a confirmation page. The person who posted the report reviews the claim. Do not claim an item that is not yours; false claims are referred to Student Affairs.

## After a claim
The person who posted the report approves or rejects the claim. When one claim is approved, other pending claims on that report are rejected automatically and the report status becomes claimed. The poster contacts you, or you message them from the report page, to arrange handover. Bring your student ID when collecting. The poster records who received the item and when, and the status becomes returned. If a claim is rejected and you believe the item is yours, contact Student Affairs with whatever evidence you have.

## Report statuses
open: waiting, nobody has claimed it. matched: a likely match exists. claimed: a claim was approved and handover is being arranged. returned: handed over to its owner. archived: closed, for example a found item nobody collected after the holding period (about 90 days by default). Archived is final.

## My Reports
Open My Reports (/my-reports) after signing in to see your reports, your claims and your bookmarks, each with its current status. You can edit a report while its status is open. To remove a report you filed by mistake, open it and click Withdraw while it is still open.

## Sign in and register
Sign in with your email address and password (CSPC addresses look like name@my.cspc.edu.ph). Google sign-in is available when the site has it turned on. To register (/register) first enter your CSPC email. We email you a 6-digit code; type it within 10 minutes (you get 5 tries per code, and you can ask for a new one after a minute). Once the code is accepted, fill in your full name, role, student or staff ID number and a password of at least 10 characters. Avoid your name, ID number or common words. Your account is then created already verified and you are signed in. If an older account was never verified, signing in sends a new code to type.

## Forgot password and sign-in problems
Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page (/forgot-password). A 6-digit code is emailed to you (valid for 10 minutes, 5 tries); type it on the next page together with your new password. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive, and use Resend code after a minute. After several wrong passwords sign-in pauses for 15 minutes; wait and try again. If the email still does not arrive or you cannot get in, visit the IT office with your student ID. The assistant cannot reset passwords and never asks for them.

## Messages and notifications
On a report page you can message the person who posted it. Each conversation is about one report. Open Messages at /messages. Notifications at /notifications show claims filed, claim decisions, status changes and new messages. Email notifications can be turned on or off from the Notifications page, and emails about the same conversation or report are spaced out so you do not get a flood.

## Bookmarks and comments
Signed-in users can bookmark a report to follow it and can comment on reports. Do not post private details such as phone numbers or ID numbers in comments.

## Matching
When a report is filed, the system looks for reports of the opposite kind (lost versus found) in the same category within a window of about 30 days. Each candidate is scored by a fixed rubric: same category, same location, close dates, and overlapping words in the title and description. The score and every reason are shown on the report page. Matching is a visible checklist, not a black box. Matches are suggestions; the report owner decides on claims.

## Nexus
Nexus (/nexus) is the campus command centre. It shows a radar of zones that still have open reports, a match scanner where you describe an item (typing or the microphone) and see matches with reasons, and a live pulse of recent reports. Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on Mac) anywhere to jump, search or send a description to the scanner.

## Handover beacon
On a report page the handover beacon is a unique sigil plus the reference number. Show it, with your student ID, when collecting an item.

## Staff and admin
Staff review guest found-item reports and approve or reject them before they become public. Staff and admin areas are not visible to ordinary users. The assistant cannot approve, reject or change anything.

## Privacy and data
WhereITis follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). It collects student number, full name, email, an optional contact number, and the report details. It does not collect gender, civil status, religion, birthday or government ID numbers. Email and contact details are not shown publicly. Reports are kept for one academic year, then archived and purged of personal data. You can see your data in My Reports, edit or withdraw open reports, and email the Data Protection Officer at dpo@cspc.edu.ph to correct, erase or export data. Details are on /privacy.

## Cookies
WhereITis sets only three short-lived cookies to keep you signed in and secure: whereitis_id (sign-in, 30 minutes), whereitis_pwreset (while resetting a password, 10 minutes) and whereitis_otp (while entering an emailed code to register, verify or reset, 30 minutes). No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Details on /cookies.

## Rules of use
Report only items you really lost or found. Do not file false claims, harass or track others, scrape the site, or post illegal or hateful content. Abuse can lead to a rejected claim, a suspended account, or referral to Student Affairs. Disputes about a claim decision go to the Student Affairs office. Details on /terms.

## Accessibility
The site aims for WCAG 2.2 Level AA, supports keyboard-only use (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, and a skip-to-content link), and respects reduced-motion settings. To report an accessibility barrier email accessibility@cspc.edu.ph or tell a staff member at the College of Computer Studies or the IT office. Details on /accessibility.

## Light and dark mode
Use the sun or moon button in the header to switch between light and dark themes.

## Where to get human help
For something this assistant cannot answer, read the user guide at /help or visit the IT office or any staff member. For rejected claims and disputes go to Student Affairs. For anything dangerous, theft in progress, harassment or an emergency, contact campus security or a staff member immediately; WhereITis is not an emergency service. The assistant has no office hours, phone numbers or fees to share.

## What the assistant can and cannot do
It can explain the steps, look up public reports by keyword or reference number, run the match scanner on a description, show site totals, and link you to the right page. It cannot file reports or claims, send messages, approve anything, change a status, reset passwords, or see private account data.
